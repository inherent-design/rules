# Versioned resource and graphics host research

This portable research contract supports a separately selected Moment intent. Build a complete, attributable map of resource-pack and graphics definitions, their actual consumers, and their domain and operational rules across selected Minecraft editions, releases, loaders, renderers, and mod-assisted variants. Reify the contracts through Bend decisions, Lean proofs, source interpretation, and appropriately bounded native evidence. Feed missing mechanisms and qualifications back into the broader native/JVM/browser/application-host study.

The destination is informed decisions about a **possible new mod combining selected roles of Modefite and Polytone**, including possible replacement of either provider in Create: Skyweave. Handpick portions later. Complete mapping is not blanket implementation or parity authority. Optimized Bushy Leaves is the trigger, not the scope or acceptance test.

## Preserve the purpose, replace the stopping condition

The [initial four-profile contract](history/four-profile-intent.md) is historical. Its vanilla 1.21.1, vanilla 1.21.5 successor, Modefite, and Modefite-plus-Compat nodes remain useful baselines; they are no longer the complete target set or final completion condition. Preserve valid [decoding](DECODING.md), [resource-selection](RESOURCES.md), and [registry discovery](REGISTRIES.md) work with its original qualifications. No inherited result automatically qualifies an expanded profile.

At activation, reconcile the old goal and continuation into this purpose, without silently running competing goals or narrowing success to completed work. The canonical running intent and checkpoint belong in Moment; this file is a reusable specification/template. Saving either does not activate native work.

## Select exact targets and independent axes

Begin with a finite target manifest and evidence cutoff. Identify edition, release, loader/API, Java/runtime, OS/build/architecture, graphics backend/driver, renderer/shader integrations, provider artifacts, configuration, and selected resource stack independently. Bind sources and artifacts immutably. A profile name, matching type name, high pack format, or mod being present is not a capability witness.

Initial required Java Edition comparison milestones are:

- **1.21.1**, the deployment baseline, including the selected NeoForge 21.1.265 environment and exact relevant provider artifacts.
- **1.21.4**, a first-class item-definition milestone whose actual introduction boundaries must be checked.
- **1.21.5**, with its own definition and consumer contracts.
- **1.21.11**, including its actual model/Euler geometry contract.
- **An exact selected 26.3 release, preview, or snapshot revision**, covering relevant shading/graphics definitions. Record actual introduction and qualification; snapshot evidence does not qualify a later release.
- **Applicable later changes** discovered through resource/consumer closure. Admit each with an exact revision, rationale, and affected contracts; a cutoff is not a promise about all future versions.

Include **Bedrock as a separate edition comparison lane** for relevant resource/graphics contracts. Select exact relevant builds during the initial pass using primary evidence and a recorded rationale. Its version numbers, files, materials, gameplay data, consumers, and renderers must not be equated with Java's. Keep inaccessible internals and unavailable native observations explicit; they do not establish either equivalence or unsupported behavior.

Map vanilla, NeoForge, Modefite, Polytone, and current Skyweave Compat feature by feature. Include reference/standalone behavior and meaningful combinations needed for current deployment, coexistence, or replacement. Do not assume all providers compose or generate an unexamined Cartesian product. Existing additive profile names may remain attributed aliases or be replaced with an explicit disposition; never interpret them as an executable union of releases.

Modefite's main item-definition role is not general modern-resource support. Inspect the supplied exact Polytone evidence, including automatic Euler decoding/baking without custom pack configuration, the absent shading-override finding, and unqualified angle/Compat overlap. Pin its artifact/configuration before consequential reliance. Inventory Polytone's other relevant domains too. These are evidence inputs, not preaccepted runtime conclusions.

## Work from sketch to linework to detail

Inventory every selected resource family, provider, consumer, actor, promise, operation, effect, and failure/recovery path before deeply elaborating one subsystem. Then map individual definitions, fields, dispatch entries, value domains, defaults, references, context requirements, and consumers. Reify bounded state/transition models across the breadth before deepening races, projection losses, version disagreements, and overbroad user promises.

Cover discovery and pack selection/consent; metadata/overlays; identifiers and physical paths; priority, merging and filters; archives/external assets; blockstates/model references; elements, rotations, normals, UVs, faces, transforms, generated geometry and culling; legacy overrides and modern item programs; predicates, selectors, numeric dispatch, components, tints and special/custom renderers; equipment/entity presentation; images, animation, mipmaps, atlases, palettes, colormaps and GUI resources; fonts/language/text/built-in lists; sounds/particles; block/biome presentation; lighting, shading, materials, shaders/includes/post-effects; render pipelines, caches, device work, reload/publication/retirement; and server-provided resources with their user/network lifecycle.

Follow dependencies into registries, stacks, entities, dimensions, biomes, clocks, inputs, actions and server authority where they affect resources. Keep unrelated systems as named boundaries. A presentation resource does not create an older host's missing item, component, mechanic, action, world state, or authoritative server datum.

## Capability and consumer contracts

For each capability or definition, retain:

1. Stable identity and edition/release/provider origin; grammar, field/value types, required/optional/absent/null distinctions, defaults, numeric representation, bounds, ordering and unknown-field policy.
2. Registration, references, lookup, stack/merge/filter, fallback, recursion/cycles and dependency rules. Keep lexical IDs separate from physical admission and selected bytes separate from decoded or verified objects.
3. Required registry/gameplay/context state, its provenance and identity, time/seed/action inputs, and whether reads are live, captured, cached, unavailable, or incomplete.
4. Outputs and actual validation/evaluation/compilation/bake/publication/render/play/apply consumers, including custom renderer and material/shader contracts.
5. Native/host calls, incidental and intended effects, ownership, visibility, ordering, acknowledgement, partial results and safe recovery. Distinguish application state, caches/logs, installed assets, user data, devices and external effects.
6. Exact source/artifact basis, evidence kind, assumptions, proofs, comparisons, counterexamples, current qualification and the observation needed to resolve each question.

Decoding, reference/context availability, compilation, baking, publication, rendering, native completion and retirement are separate obligations. Unknown or ignored fields are not applied semantics. A loaded pack, successful parser call, registry count or plausible screenshot does not establish whole-pack equivalence.

Use typed graph edges for content references, retaining roots/leases, lineage/attribution, registration, context reads, invalidation and publication. Not every reference retains content or grants authority. Carry source/profile identity through codecs and relations so similar values from incompatible domains are not silently interchangeable.

## Fidelity and possible future architecture

Classify each capability/profile result as a faithful semantic backport candidate, asset-conversion candidate under stated restrictions, explicitly selected approximation, demonstrated unsupported case, or unknown/incomplete case. State the observable contract and preserved/lost semantics. Conversion must not conceal a runtime approximation. Missing gameplay data or a custom renderer requires its own disposition, not fabricated defaults.

Produce independently selectable subsystem proposals and dependency closures for the possible new mod. Compare provider extension, coexistence, replacement, conversion, and keeping current behavior. Explain what changes and why, what stays native, what requires new gameplay/rendering implementation, compatibility breaks, preservation requirements and falsifiable qualification checks. Owner acceptance and implementation remain later decisions.

A clean break from existing Minecraft-specific abstractions is authorized where this research needs it. Transform or delete obsolete definitions, codecs and proofs rather than preserving a wrong model through compatibility layers. First inspect their consumers and classify claims as retained, narrowed, replaced, withdrawn or historical; migrate references/checks coherently and preserve consequential original evidence and counterexamples. Do not rewrite measured outcomes to fit new rules. Changes to shared non-Minecraft contracts need evidence from affected consumers and owner coordination.

Bend and Lean serve modeling and verification here. An in-game Bend runtime, JNI bridge, callable engine, GPU implementation or production mod is not selected by a useful theorem or prototype.

## Coexistence, scheduling and lifetime

Give every parser, loader hook, transform, definition table, cache, bake output, renderer/material attachment and published generation an owner. Model physical-side/version/configuration gates, hook order, overlap, invalidation, fallback responsibility and conflicts. Qualify Modefite/Polytone/Compat geometry and angle interactions instead of assuming that enabling capable providers composes them. Prevent double transforms/application, stale tables and ambiguous fallback ownership.

Cover preparation barriers, listener order, partial apply, async results, stale work, cancellation, worker loss, disconnect, device/context generations and recovery. State only the atomicity/retention the actual owner supplies. UI closure, cancellation requests, one completed submission, a dropped view or an error do not prove global rollback or final reclamation. Track old snapshots, in-flight work, caches, native/GPU handles and independent consumers until their obligations retire.

Lazy lookup/read is not an immutable snapshot. Distinguish concurrent changes, content/metadata coordination and late receipts from a before/after image check. Bind publication and approval to current target, basis, owner and relevant revision. Preserve uncertainty when acknowledgement is lost or effects may already exist.

## Operational UX

Derive human and machine reports from the same facts, with stable identifiers. Distinguish recognized, selected, decoded, applied, converted, ignored, refused, partial and unknown. Name affected resources, provider/version/backend/data requirements, conflict owner, useful cause/consequence and safe next action.

Preserve stack order, user choice/consent, drafts, focus, accessibility and recoverable state where the real consumer permits. Keep diagnostics available after transient notifications. Do not say saved, installed, applied, compatible, cancelled, recovered or verified-rendered from weaker evidence. Missing data, backend, schema support and provider conflicts need specific dispositions. Proposals support per-capability review; reports do not enable features, mutate packs or grant runtime access.

## Update the broader hosts alongside Minecraft

Continue the six common surfaces: files/storage; UI/input/processes; concurrency/scheduling/time; networking/subscriptions; resources/devices/completion; authority/human interaction. Minecraft findings must update shared contracts and relevant native/JVM/browser/application handoffs when their assumptions change.

Revalidate applicable named Windows 11 servicing/Win32 profiles, macOS/Darwin profiles, GNU/Linux LTS/kernel/libc profiles, and actual builds, architectures, API layers, filesystems and drivers. Record support/maintenance at the selected cutoff. Windows Server CI does not qualify Windows 11. Keep native, virtualized, emulated, cross-compiled, source-only and simulated evidence distinct.

Retain Java **17 as the common host baseline with 21 and 25 overlays**; do not reintroduce Java 18. Minecraft targets use their actual required/qualified runtime: the generic Java 17 baseline does not make Minecraft run on Java 17. Browser/native-desktop and future mobile/embedded boundaries stay available where actual consumers require them, without inventing a universal platform API.

Identify actual graphics APIs/backends/drivers independently of abstract device interfaces. Do not infer a native backend from an abstraction. Propagate relevant updates through profiles, codecs, rules, evidence, documentation and consumer handoffs. Updating host models does not authorize automatic OS, JDK, application or daemon upgrades.

## Evidence, verification and custody

Use primary versioned sources and exact artifacts. Distinguish vanilla, loader-patched and provider code. Pin revisions, dirty working-tree identities, binary/mapping/library/tool digests, configurations, selected assets and environments. Inspect consequential mc-modpacks handoffs. Keep extracted candidates, inspected semantics, original-method execution, source-fragment execution with adapters, simulation, native reload/rendering, model agreement and proposals separate.

Decompiled text is an inspection aid, not translation equivalence. Resolve suspicious reconstructed capture/type/control flow against bytecode or actual methods. Preserve failed observers and dispositions separately from product outcomes. Retain attributable inputs, producer, basis, actual commands, outputs, effects and tested snapshot.

Use Bend for executable decisions/permitted traces and Lean for invariants with named premises. Include malformed, missing, conflicting, unsupported, stale, boundary, interrupted and changed-observation controls capable of detecting wrong models/codecs. Compare meaningful actual consumer boundaries where feasible. Shared adapter assumptions remain explicit. Compiler success, constant examples and test/source-entry counts do not prove breadth or translation fidelity.

Keep original proprietary sources/binaries, artwork, credentials, accounts, personal instances, worlds and recovery material out of public Git. Use bounded isolated generated fixtures and attributable private inputs. Coordinate actual graphical desktop actions through re:desktop immediately before use. Preserve other writers' work. Research does not select live provider/pack installation, production backports, personal rollout, publishing/deployment or irreversible user-state changes.

Ordinary CI runs rules/proofs, retained comparisons and bounded host fixtures, never studied applications/games/browsers, inference or personal/remote services. Select local native evidence explicitly and qualify missing platforms honestly. Run meaningful changed-claim checks, review combined diffs/consumers, coordinate scoped commits/pushes with the application owner and batch remote CI instead of running it per small band.

Bound download/extraction/VM/cache allocation and concurrency. Preserve active/current required inputs or the latest two completed items per category, not an age-window union. Any retirement needs applicable authority, known consumers/writers, consequential evidence, restoration limits and measured allocation/free-space qualification. This intent does not activate unrelated storage/maintenance work.

## Deliverables and completion audit

Completion requires all of these for the selected target set:

1. A finite edition/release/loader/runtime/backend/provider/configuration manifest with rationale, immutable identities, meaningful variants and explicit unavailable targets.
2. A source/asset-derived inventory of every relevant definition family and consumer closure, including unchanged, unavailable and refused branches. Material target-selection or inventory omissions prevent completion.
3. Attributable field/type/default/value/reference/merge/failure/operation semantics and typed dependency/effect/lifetime relationships. Material unknowns remain incomplete, not silently supported or unsupported.
4. Shared/refined Bend and Lean models with named assumptions, executable examples, counterexamples, checked proofs and meaningful native/controlled/retained comparisons for the claimed boundaries, with projection and model limits visible.
5. Cross-version/provider analysis separating faithful semantics, restricted conversion, selected approximation, unsupported and unknown, including absent older gameplay data and custom renderer/backend obligations.
6. Explicit coexistence/ownership, startup gates, reload/publication/retirement and UX contracts with partial/uncertain outcomes and safe recovery. Parsing or compilation does not prove global rollback or rendering.
7. Independently selectable proposals for a possible combined mod, with dependency closure, alternatives, compatibility breaks, preservation rules, qualification checks and remaining owner decisions. Implementation remains unstarted unless separately selected.
8. Relevant changes/evidence in the broader native/JVM/browser/device host study and coordinated downstream handoffs, without treating one runner as all-platform qualification.
9. Coherent public docs/navigation, source/evidence ledgers, clean-break dispositions, preserved consequential evidence, adequate local verification and one coordinated rules-only cross-platform CI result for the published snapshot.
10. A requirement-by-requirement final audit of current artifacts and evidence. Missing, uncertain, indirect or contradicted evidence does not establish completion. Notify the owner only when this expanded stated scope is satisfied; report material qualifications honestly.

This replaces the four-profile stopping condition while preserving the underlying research purpose. It grants no blanket implementation authority and activates no Moment core changes or other projects' goals.
